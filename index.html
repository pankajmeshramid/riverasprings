The Source

Pure water from the dense forests of Pangdi and Vidarbha’s pristine waterfalls, not a municipal borewell

Rivera Springs draws from the pure, untouched waterfalls and natural springs in the dense forests of Pangdi and Vidarbha — water naturally filtered through ancient rock formations over millennia. Rich in Magnesium, Potassium & Sodium, this water carries the balanced mineral profile that only geological filtration can provide.

This isn’t treated municipal water or borewell supply dressed up with a label. This is nature’s own filtration, bottled at the source. We pipe from spring to plant within a closed circuit — no tanker transport, no open-air holding. The bottling facility sits at the source, not hundreds of kilometres away in an industrial estate.

Quality & Certification

Tested at source, tested at line, tested before dispatch

Every production batch is sampled and tested against parameters mandated by IS 14543:2016. Records are maintained for FSSAI inspection on demand.

Sourcing

Natural springs in the dense forests of Pangdi and Vidarbha. Water surfaces through ancient rock formations that impart a balanced mineral profile — Magnesium, Potassium, Sodium and Calcium in naturally occurring proportions. TDS maintained between 50–120 ppm.

Processing

Multi-stage treatment as printed on every label: Sand Filtration, Carbon Filtration, Reverse Osmosis, UV Treatment & Ozonisation. Added minerals: Magnesium, Potassium & Sodium. Fully automated bottling line — no manual handling from intake to sealed cap.

Certification

FSSAI Licensed (Lic No: 11526999000410). IS 14543 compliant. ISO 22000 food safety management. BIS certification mark on every bottle. Manufactured & marketed by Maya International Exports, Gat No. 407/1/B, Tanda, Tal & Dist Gondia, Maharashtra 441614.

Founder’s Journey

Saurabh K

Role Founder, Rivera Springs
Education IIIT Nagpur
Background Investment Banking, Trading, Real Estate
Base Amgaon, Maharashtra

Saurabh K built his early career far from the water industry — as an investment banker, an active trader, and a real estate entrepreneur. That background shaped how he approaches Rivera Springs: with the same discipline he’d bring to any serious venture.

“I spent years reading markets that lie to you constantly. Water doesn’t get that luxury. Ours never will.”

— Saurabh K, Founder

When he turned to packaged drinking water, it wasn’t a departure from that discipline — it was an application of it. He studied the industry the way he’d study any market: sourcing, filtration technology, quality benchmarks, regulatory compliance, before committing capital or reputation to it. He founded Maya International Exports and set up the Rivera Springs bottling line near Amgaon — deliberately close to the source rather than close to the market.

What drives him is a preoccupation with water quality that goes beyond mere compliance — every batch tested, every source monitored, every deviation investigated.
